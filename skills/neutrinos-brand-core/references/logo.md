# Logo

The Neutrinos logo is crafted custom art and **is not to be altered in any way.**
Always place one of the bundled files. Do not redraw, recolor, re-space, or
re-typeset it.

## Anatomy

- **Symbol** - the angled "neutrino" mark. Inspired by neutrinos (extremely fast
  fundamental particles); it stands for **speed** and being **hyper-focused** on
  accelerating customers' transformation.
- **Logotype** - the "Neutrinos" wordmark (drawn from TT Norms Pro).
- **Tagline** - "Reinvent. Accelerated." (optional, horizontal lockup only).

## Configurations

| Configuration | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| **Horizontal** (primary) | Default. Use whenever possible. With or without tagline. |
| **Vertical** | Narrow/tall layouts; social media profile icon; merch. **Never with the tagline.** |
| **Symbol** only | Very small sizes, favicons, app icons, avatars, watermark/supergraphic. |

## Color versions

Choose the version that suits the background:

- **Full color** (blue symbol + black wordmark) on **White** or **Mist Gray** - primary.
- **Full color with white wordmark** on **Neutrinos Blue** or **Midnight Blue**.
- **All white** on any dark/photographic background.
- **All black** when full color is unavailable (1-color print, faxes, etc.).

## Clear space

Keep a minimum of **1/4 X** clear space on all sides, where **X = the width of the
Neutrinos symbol**. Nothing (text, images, edges) intrudes into that space.

## Minimum size

- With tagline: **25 px** high (~0.347 in).
- Without tagline: **10 px** high (~0.1 in).
- Measure by the height of the Neutrinos symbol.

## Tagline lock-up

- Set in **Poppins Medium**, upper and lower case.
- **Same color as the logotype - never colored.**
- No taller than **1/3** of the logotype height.
- Offset from the logotype by **>= 1X** (horizontal) or **>= 1/2 X** (vertical).
- Tagline may also be used on its own, aligned horizontally or vertically.

## Product logos

Product names can lock up to the symbol (e.g. **Neutrinos Trinity**). Set both the
"Neutrinos" and the product name in **Poppins Medium**, upper and lower case,
following the symbol construction proportions. Any approved logo color version may
be used.

## The eight "incorrect uses" (do NOT)

1. Do not use the **old** Neutrinos logo.
2. Do not **alter the size or placement** of the logo elements.
3. Do not **extend the symbol's dynamic lines** when it is locked up as a logo
   (line extension is only for the Supergraphic).
4. Do not **italicize or angle** the logo.
5. Do not add a **drop shadow**.
6. Do not **stretch or distort** it.
7. Do not add **color to the tagline**.
8. Do not **change the logo color** or use an off-brand color.

Also never: rotate it, place it on a low-contrast/busy background, re-create it in
a different font, or add effects (gradients, outlines, glows).
